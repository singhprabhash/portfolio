## Brand & Style

The design system translates the physical gravitas, typographic discipline, and ink-on-stock tactility of high-end independent editorial publishing into an interactive portfolio medium. It merges Swiss International Typographic Style with tactile print materiality, subverting conventional digital portfolio templates in favor of broadsheet tension, asymmetric column structures, and strict structural hairline rules.

### Design Movement & Core Philosophy
- **Modernist Broadsheet Editorial:** Uncompromising hierarchy, deliberate scale friction between gargantuan grotesque titles, high-contrast literary serifs, and raw technical monospace annotations.
- **Physical Materiality:** Grounded on a textured warm paper canvas rather than digital white, treating elements not as glowing screens, but as mechanical ink impressions, offset printing stamps, and publication folios.
- **Micro-Mechanical Precision:** Structural boundaries are articulated through razor-thin 1px hairline rules, indexing markers, registration crosshairs, and dynamic issue/spread metadata numbering.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authentic paper-and-ink tension: dense charcoal impressions set over a tactile, warm ivory foundation, punctured by high-visibility editorial signaling pigments.

### Functional Palette Roles
- **Background / Paper Ivory (`#F5F0E8`):** The primary canvas substrate. Simulates high-grade unbleached cotton paper stock.
- **Ink Black / Deep Charcoal (`#171717`):** The primary printing ink. Used for high-impact headlines, body text, structural rules, and inverted solid blocks.
- **Acid Lime (`#D7FF64`):** Primary kinetic accent. Applied sparingly to interactive focus states, critical navigation triggers, project status tags, and tactile highlight markers.
- **Signal Red (`#E45B43`):** Editorial alert, section indexing numbers, and errata/archive stamps. Evokes classic offset editorial registration markers.
- **Muted Taupe (`#958C80`):** Secondary metadata, caption details, author notes, and secondary column descriptions.
- **Surface White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-light paper inserts, tipped-in card plates, and elevated project framing.
- **Hairline Rules (`#D8D0C4`):** 1px structural framing and grid divisions; sits tone-on-tone with `#F5F0E8` to mirror blind debossing and fine metal ruling.

## Typography

Typography functions as the primary visual architecture. It stages a deliberate conflict between three distinct typographic voices:
1. **The Modernist Grotesk (`Space Grotesk`):** Dominates macro hierarchy, titles, case study titles, and primary reading copy. Tight tracking and compressed leading recreate editorial Swiss lead-type setting.
2. **The Literary Editorial Serif (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved exclusively for pull-quotes, personal introspections, manifestos, and italicized editorial asides.
3. **The Mechanical Monospace (`Space Mono`):** Dictates layout metadata, coordinates, article indices, timestamps, project parameters, and publication numbering. Always uppercase for small labels and tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on an asymmetric 12-column grid system reminiscent of broadsheet magazines. Content intentionally breaks symmetry: text may consume 5 columns while leaving 2 columns empty before a full 5-column media plate.

### Breakpoints & Grid Structuring
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Asymmetric grouping (e.g., Col 1-3 Index, Col 4-9 Narrative, Col 10-12 Artifact Metadata).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 6-column grid with `2rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Side rails collapse into sequential modular rows separated by horizontal hairline dividers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked broadsheet layout with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Strict vertical cadence delineated by top and bottom borders.

### Rules of Alignment
- Empty canvas is never accidental; negative space serves as an editorial margin for metadata markers.
- Continuous 1px hairline rules (`#D8D0C4`) span the full bleed of content containers horizontally and vertically, anchoring grid columns to the edge of the screen.

## Elevation & Depth

In line with traditional print design, this system avoids faux drop shadows, blur radii, and floating Z-axis elevations. Depth is purely graphic, achieved through:

- **Surface Tiers:** Layering `#FFFFFF` panels directly over `#F5F0E8` canvas substrates to emulate tipped-in plates and print inserts.
- **Offset Inking & High-Contrast Overlays:** Deep charcoal (`#171717`) blocks dropped squarely onto ivory surfaces with hard boundaries.
- **Physical Hairline Boundaries:** 1px solid borders (`#D8D0C4` or `#171717`) define containment. No soft ambient shadows or atmospheric glows are permitted.
- **Visual Stamping:** Accents (`#D7FF64` and `#E45B43`) create focal planes by appearing as physical ink overlays and risograph-inspired registration tags.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture is uncompromisingly sharp (`0px` corner radius across all elements). Every surface, button, tag, input field, and visual container conforms to rigid right angles, maintaining the physical cut of paper stock, printing blocks, and metal plate typography.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Button:** Solid `#171717` rectangle, `#F5F0E8` typography set in `Space Mono` uppercase. Hover transitions instantly (0ms or 100ms linear) to `#D7FF64` background with `#171717` text.
- **Secondary / Hairline Button:** Transparent background, 1px solid `#171717` border, `#171717` text. Hover shifts to solid `#171717` fill with `#F5F0E8` text.
- **Editorial Action Link:** Underlined with a crisp 1px rule offset by 4px. Accompanied by a monospace directional arrow `[ -> ]`.

### Chips & Metadata Badges
- Strict rectangular containers with 1px border in `#D8D0C4` or `#171717`.
- Typeset in `meta-mono-bold` uppercase with wide letter spacing.
- Status/Active variant features a solid `#D7FF64` fill with `#171717` text, signaling dynamic editorial status (e.g., `[ ARCHIVE // 2024 ]`, `[ LIVE SYSTEM ]`).

### Cards & Project Folios
- Outlined with 1px hairline rule `#D8D0C4`. 
- Features an editorial header row: top-left monospace index number (e.g., `NO. 004 / FOLIO`), top-right client or timestamp in `meta-technical`.
- Media images sit flush within borders without padding or rounded corners.
- Interactive hover: The perimeter rule shifts from `#D8D0C4` to `#171717`, with subtle typographic inversion.

### Lists & Case Studies Index
- Rendered as an index table separated by 1px horizontal hairline rules.
- Columns explicitly split into: Index (`#E45B43`), Project Title (`Space Grotesk` medium), Discipline/Role (`#958C80`), and Year (`Space Mono`).
- Hovering a row triggers a sharp inverse flash or displays a thumbnail preview anchored to the cursor.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Unboxed or bottom-border-only baseline styling using 1px `#171717` or `#D8D0C4` rule.
- Field labels float above in `meta-mono-bold` uppercase.
- Focus state activates a sharp 1px solid `#171717` lower border paired with a `#D7FF64` indicator dot.

### Editorial Elements
- **Pull Quote Module:** Sits across multiple columns, framed by top and bottom 1px rules in `#171717`, typeset in `Playfair Display` italic, anchored with a `#E45B43` footnote reference.
- **Colophon / Metadata Block:** Fixed technical block at section terminals containing typography specs, coordinate grids, and release timestamps.
